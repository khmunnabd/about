The journey

Creative work with a human point of view.

Across education, creative production and development-focused storytelling, each role has strengthened my ability to communicate with clarity, empathy and visual purpose.

Institutional storytelling & brand communication

At International School Dhaka, I worked within the marketing team to create photography, video and digital content supporting the school’s brand communication. I contributed to maintaining a consistent visual language across campaigns and institutional storytelling. At Daffodil International University, I lead and support media production for university branding, promotional activities and events.

Documentary & community-focused storytelling

My earlier production work included projects in the Rohingya response context and visual documentation involving children with disabilities. These experiences shaped my approach to ethical, people-centred storytelling — using images and film to represent real experiences with care and dignity.
