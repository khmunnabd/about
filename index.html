The journey

Creative work with a human point of view.

Across institutional communications, creative production and development-focused storytelling, each experience has strengthened my ability to communicate with clarity, empathy and visual purpose.

Brand communication & institutional storytelling

At International School Dhaka, I worked with the marketing team to create photography, video and digital content supporting the school’s brand communication. At Daffodil International University, I lead and coordinate media production for university branding, promotional activities and events.

NGO, documentary & community storytelling

My earlier work includes visual production in the Rohingya response context and documentation involving children with disabilities. These experiences shaped my approach to ethical, people-centred storytelling — representing real experiences with care, dignity and purpose.
